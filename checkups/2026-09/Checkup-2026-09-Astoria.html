September Check-up · Astoria · Form IB

Covers Weeks 1–4 (Sep 9 – Oct 3, 2026) · AO Year 2 Term 1 · two short sittings, about 20–30 minutes each

What this isA gentle, Charlotte Mason–style “exam”: Astoria tells back what she knows, does a few sums, writes her best line, and recites. Nothing here is new material; every question comes from September’s lessons. It is not a graded test and there are no letter grades, percentages, or percentiles. The scores just show us, month by month, what is settling in.

Day 1 · about 30 min

  • Narration: Literature & Tales (L1–L4)
  • Narration: History (H1–H3)
  • Narration: Bible, by reference (B1–B3)
  • Math: oral, then written (M1–M9)

Suggested: Friday of Week 5 (Oct 9), or any day Hallie picks.

Day 2 · about 25 min

  • Narration: Geography (G1–G2)
  • Narration: Science & Nature (S1–S2)
  • Copywork + dictation (C1, D1)
  • Recitation (R1–R2)
  • Riches, optional (A1–A4)

Suggested: Saturday of Week 5 (Oct 10).

ScoreMeansWhat it looks like
3ConfidentlyOn her own, in order, with real detail (names, places, what happened). Math right without help. Neat, careful letters.
2MostlyThe main idea and some detail; a small slip or one gentle prompt (“And then?”).
1With helpBits and pieces, or only with several prompts or a hint. Math right only after you walk through it.
0Not yetDoesn’t remember it yet, or it wasn’t reached. That is fine; it just tells us what to revisit.
How to give it. Read each prompt as written, then wait. Don’t correct during a narration. Jot two or three key points she says in the score sheet’s notes column (pages 7–8), then circle 0–3. If something wasn’t actually covered, write “n/a” instead of a score. Stop early if she’s tired; finish the next day.

Bible: references only. Open your ESV to the chapter if she wants to look; no Bible text is printed here. Tracy (2): no check-up; she may sit nearby with crayons or McGuffey pictures and join the songs.

  • Each month’s totals go on Astoria’s trajectory, so we can see the trend.
  • Low items become next month’s review ideas, never extra drills.
Not a standardized test. These are home check-ups written from our own lessons. They show Astoria’s own trend over the year. They can’t be turned into percentiles or compared with Texas or national averages. For that, the plan is one nationally normed test at the end of the school year (spring 2027).
