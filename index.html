How to teach

Short lessons

  • Read once. Close the book. They narrate.
  • Do not prompt. Wait.
  • Stop at 15–20 minutes, while they still want more.
  • One line of copywork. Best handwriting. Stop.
  • If it falls apart: a song and a walk still count.
